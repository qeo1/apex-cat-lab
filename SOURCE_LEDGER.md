## Authenticity labels

- A: official primary examination authority or document.
- B: reproduction directly traceable to a specific official response sheet or answer key.
- C: institutional or established-archive transcription without question-by-question official trace established here.
- D: memory-based reconstruction.
- E: unverified reconstruction.

No question has been admitted into the app as an authentic PYQ. The app's three sample prompts are original examples and are not labeled as CAT questions.
